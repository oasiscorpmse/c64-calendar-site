C64 Calendar Privacy Policy

C64 Calendar is a personal-use application that accesses Google Calendar data for the purpose of displaying and managing calendar events for the authorized user.

Google Calendar Data

The application accesses Google Calendar information only after the user grants permission through Google's OAuth authorization process.

Google Calendar data is used only to provide calendar functions requested by the authorized user.

Storage

OAuth authorization credentials and tokens are stored locally on the user's Linux computer. Calendar information may be processed locally as necessary to display or modify calendar events.

Sharing

Google Calendar data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated third parties.

Revoking Access

The user may revoke the application's access to Google Calendar at any time through the user's Google Account permissions.

Changes

This privacy policy will be updated if the application changes the way it accesses, uses, stores, or shares Google user data.
